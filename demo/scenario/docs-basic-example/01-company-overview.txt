ACME WIDGETS — COMPANY OVERVIEW

Acme Widgets manufactures ergonomic desk accessories. Founded 2018, headquarters in Lyon, France.
Employees: 85. Primary market: EU B2B office supplies.

Revenue FY2024 (audited): EUR 12M. EBITDA margin: 18%.

Claim: Acme is profitable with stable margins.
Goal: Complete a lightweight supplier onboarding review.
Risk: Single-source chip supplier for smart desk mat controllers.
