# Dorigo 2000 vs 2024: Which Reference to Use

The codebase and docs refer to two different Dorigo-related papers. Here is the difference and how to cite.

## Dorigo et al. (2000) — in `references.bib` as `dorigo2000`

- **Full citation:** Dorigo, M., Bonabeau, E., & Theraulaz, G. (2000). Ant Algorithms and Stigmergy. *Future Generation Computer Systems*, 16(8), 851–871. doi:[10.1016/S0167-739X(00)00042-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-739X(00)00042-X)
- **Content:** Foundational treatment of **stigmergy** (indirect coordination via environment, e.g. pheromone trails) and **ant-colony optimization**. Defines the paradigm: agents modify a shared environment; others react to those modifications without direct messaging.
- **Use when:** You need a citation for stigmergy, pressure-directed activation, or environment-as-signal coordination. The paper and the .tex use this for convergence-driven agent routing and the “pressure map as gradient” idea.

## Dorigo, Theraulaz, Trianni (2024) — cited in docs/README only

- **Claimed citation:** Dorigo, M., Theraulaz, G., & Trianni, V. (2024). Swarm Intelligence: Past, Present, and Future. *Proceedings of the Royal Society B*, 291(2024). doi:10.1098/rspb.2024.0856
- **Status:** The DOI 10.1098/rspb.2024.0856 returns 404 (checked 2026-03). No matching Royal Society B 2024 paper was found for that exact title/authors/DOI. A 2021 IEEE survey by Dorigo, Theraulaz, and Trianni on *swarm robotics* exists (“Swarm Robotics: Past, Present, and Future”), but venue and year differ.
- **Use when:** Until the 2024 Royal Society B reference is verified or corrected, rely on **Dorigo et al. (2000)** for stigmergy and swarm-intelligence foundations. If you need a “past, present, future” survey, use the 2021 IEEE paper or verify the correct Royal Society B citation.

## Recommendation

- For **stigmergy and pressure-directed activation:** cite **Dorigo et al. (2000)** (`dorigo2000` in the .bib). This is what the main paper uses and it is correct.
- For **“Swarm Intelligence: Past, Present, and Future” (2024):** remove or replace the current doc/README citation until the Royal Society B reference is confirmed. Optionally add the 2021 Dorigo–Theraulaz–Trianni IEEE survey to the .bib if you want a survey citation.
